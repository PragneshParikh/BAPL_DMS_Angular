BGAUSS VEHICLE DELIVERY CHECKLIST FOR DEALER
Date: {{ currentDate | date:'dd-MM-yyyy' }}
1. Has the PDI been completed & test trial taken? Is vehicle static and dynamic performance OK?
2. Has the Battery been fully charged? and Tyre Pressure as per norms?
3. Has the coupon book been duly filled & Coupon serial no. kept on record in the sales register and update to DMS?
4. Has the Delivery Certificate (DC) from the coupon and DMS been filled (DMS DC should be signed and uploaded to DMS/Coupon DC to be filled, signed removed and filed in dealer records.)
5. Have you educated the customer on all below aspects:
a. Physically shown and explained in details operating controls of the eScooter to the customer.
b. Briefed customer in details on policy of services, warranty and maintenance schedule.
c. Inform about recommended tyre pressure, charger operation and tips to take care of the vehicle & battery .
d. Customer or his representative inspect/ test drive the scooter at the time of delivery and he should confirm it is free from any operational/visual defects.
e. The dealer should handover 2 keys along with 2 key fobs, first aid kit and tool kit along with the eScooter.
f. Customer should be assisted in downloading the mobile customer app and complete the registration process on his mobile with registered mobile number.
g. Delivery certificate from thee DMS, should be printed and signed off.This gets printed along with the invoice and is the second page.
